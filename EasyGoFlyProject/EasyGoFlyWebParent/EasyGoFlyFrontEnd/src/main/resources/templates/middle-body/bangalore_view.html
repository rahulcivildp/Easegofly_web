Package Overview

Bengaluru, formerly known as Bangalore, serves as the seat of government for the Indian state of Karnataka. It is located in southern India on the Deccan Plateau. In the city, modernization and tradition coexist. The city's different ethnic communities participate in the city's burgeoning café and pub scene as they observe their own national holidays. Since it is the hub of Indian IT development, "Silicon Valley" is a common nickname for the area.
